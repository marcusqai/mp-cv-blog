### Overview

This video presentation delivers a comprehensive executive briefing on the **Cost of Quality (CoQ)** — one of the most consequential yet underutilized frameworks in quality management and supply chain operations.

Drawing from the classic PAF model (Prevention, Appraisal, Failure), the presentation bridges engineering practice with financial logic, making the business case for proactive quality investment accessible to every leader in the room.

### What This Video Covers

**The PAF Framework** — Understanding how quality costs divide into three categories:

- **Prevention (P):** Supplier audits, process planning, preventive maintenance. Every dollar invested here prevents exponentially larger downstream losses.
- **Appraisal (A):** Incoming inspections, equipment calibration. Appraisal finds problems — but it does not eliminate them.
- **Failure (F):** Internal failures (scrap, rework) and external failures after shipment. External failure costs are often **10 to 100 times higher** than internal failures.

**The 1:10:100 Quality Leverage Effect** — The central ROI equation:

> Every  invested in Prevention eliminates 0 in Appraisal and prevents 00 in Failure costs.

**Supply Chain Transformation** — The framework challenges supply chain leaders to shift from a **Police force** (auditing to find faults) to a **Coach** (partnering with suppliers to prevent non-conformance).

**Operational Tactics** — Concrete engineering activities that operationalize the coach methodology: process capability studies, quality system implementation, and joint root cause analysis.

### Who This Is For

This presentation is designed for **quality managers, supply chain directors, and executive leaders** who want to:

- Connect quality investments directly to financial outcomes
- Build the business case for proactive quality spending
- Understand the PAF model through a strategic, not just tactical, lens
- Lead supplier development programs with data-backed ROI arguments

### 概述

本影片演示提供了一份全面的品質成本高管簡報——品質管理與供應鏈運營中最具影響力卻最常被忽視的框架之一。

演示借鑽經典的PAF模型（預防、鑑定、失敗），將工程實踐與財務邏輯相結合，讓每位領導者都能理解主動式品質投資的商業價值。

### 影片內容

**PAF框架** — 理解品質成本如何分為三類：

- **預防（P）：** 供應商審核、製程規劃、預防性維護。在此投入的每一元，都能防止指數級的更大損失。
- **鑑定（A）：** 進料檢驗、設備校準。鑑定能發現問題——但不能消除問題。
- **失敗（F）：** 內部失敗（廢料、重工）和出貨後的外部失敗。外部失敗成本通常是內部的**10到100倍**。

**1:10:100品質槓桿效應** — 核心ROI公式：

> 每1元預防投資可消除10元鑑定成本，並防止100元失敗成本。

**供應鏈轉型** — 框架要求供應鏈領導者從「警察」角色轉向「教練」：從審核找錯，轉變為與供應商合作預防不合格品。

**執行方法** — 實施教練方法論的具體工程活動：製程能力研究、品質系統建置、聯合根本原因分析。

### 適用對象

本演示專為希望達成以下目標的**品質經理、供應鏈總監和高層領導者**設計：

- 將品質投資與財務成果直接掛鉤
- 建立主動式品質支出的商業案例
- 從戰略而非戰術的角度理解PAF模型
- 用有數據支撐的ROI論據領導供應商開發計劃
