## Mary Barra's Culture Transformation at General Motors

🏭 In 2014, Mary Barra became CEO of General Motors — the first woman to lead a major global automaker. Less than a year into her tenure, she was hit with the ignition switch recall crisis, affecting 30 million vehicles.

The previous management chose to cover it up. Mary chose a completely different path.

Instead of hiring lawyers to bury the truth, she personally met with the victims' families, took full responsibility, and said these famous words:

> _"We need to fundamentally change our culture, because culture was the real problem here."_

### Three Things She Did

1. **Publicly acknowledged the mistakes** — no blame-shifting, no deflection.
2. **Launched the "Speak Up for Safety" program** — empowering employees to report safety concerns directly, bypassing their superiors if needed.
3. **Completely transformed the company culture** — from "only report good news" to "finding problems early makes you a hero."

### The Result

GM's reputation was restored. The company successfully transitioned to electric vehicles — the Hummer EV and Silverado EV. Mary Barra was named one of TIME's 100 Most Influential People.

💡 **The Lesson:** Culture eats strategy for breakfast. When problems arise, the instinct is to find scapegoats or downplay the situation. But truly great leaders step forward and say "This is my responsibility" — then fix the **culture**, not just the product.

## Mary Barra 改造通用汽車文化

🏭 2014年，Mary Barra 成為通用汽車（GM）CEO，也是全球大型車廠首位女性掌門人。上任不到一年，就碰上點火開關召回危機，影響三千萬輛車。

前任管理層選擇隱瞞，但 Mary 選擇了完全不同的路——

她沒有找律師掩蓋真相，而是親自會見受害者家屬，承擔全部責任，並說了一句著名的話：

> _"We need to fundamentally change our culture, because culture was the real problem here."_

### 她做了三件事

1. **公開承認錯誤** — 不推卸責任，不找藉口。
2. **發起「Speak Up for Safety」計畫** — 讓員工可以越過上級直接反映安全問題。
3. **徹底改變公司文化** — 從「報喜不報憂」變成「早發現問題才是英雄」。

### 結果

GM 信譽恢復，成功轉型電動車（Hummer EV、Silverado EV），Mary Barra 入選時代百大影響力人物。

💡 **啟示：** Culture eats strategy for breakfast. 當問題發生時，直覺反應是找替罪羊或淡化處理。但真正偉大的領導者會站出來說「這是我的責任」，然後去修復**文化**，而不只是修復產品。
