## Habit 1: Extreme Information Filtering — Eliminate 99% of Noise
## 習慣 1：建立極度的信息過濾比例（剔除 99% 無效信息）

**The Claim:** 注意力是最寶貴資產，甚至超過金錢。關掉不必要通知，退出無意義群聊，遠離消耗你的人和事。

**In English:** Attention is your most valuable asset — even more than money. Turn off unnecessary notifications, leave meaningless group chats, and stay away from people and things that drain your energy.

### Fact Check

| Claim | Verdict |
|:---|:---|
| Attention is the most valuable asset | ✅ **Verified** |
| Short videos drain attention | ✅ **Verified** |
| "99% useless information" | ⚠️ **Rhetorical exaggeration** — not a precise figure |

**Research:** Herbert Simon (1971) coined "attention economy": information richness leads to attention poverty. Gloria Mark (UC Irvine) found people switch attention every 47 seconds and need 23 minutes to refocus after interruption.

### Commentary
✅ **Correct direction** — Attention management is genuinely high-ROI.
⚠️ **Overly extreme** — Complete elimination of entertainment may lead to social isolation and burnout.
⚠️ **Ignores information breadth** — Sometimes "useless" information triggers cross-domain innovation.

### 🎯 Practical Application at Work

Your daily work involves: Supplier Quality Reports, Daily Evolution, Blog posts, Cron management.

**Concrete Actions:**
1. **Turn off all non-urgent notifications** — Keep only Telegram (boss's messages) and system alerts
2. **Set "Deep Work Hours"** — 08:00-10:00 for writing reports, no non-urgent replies
3. **Information source grading:**
   - 🟢 Must-read: Boss messages, Supplier reports, Cron errors
   - 🟡 Optional: Industry news, Blog comments
   - 🔴 Don't read: Entertainment gossip, meaningless social media
4. **Telegram group management** — Mute all non-critical groups, keep only Direct Message alerts

## Habit 2: Result-Oriented Reverse Engineering
## 習慣 2：結果導向的逆向工程（以終為始）

**The Claim:** 太多人「用戰術勤奮掩蓋戰略懶惰」——打卡背單詞、假裝加班，結果一無所有。真正的高手先鎖定目標，再冷酷反推關鍵步驟，砍掉一切不能導向結果的動作。商業社會只認結果不認苦勞。

**In English:** Too many people "use tactical diligence to掩盖 strategic laziness" —打卡 vocabulary, pretend overtime, yet achieve nothing. True top performers lock onto their goal first, then coldly reverse-engineer the key steps, cutting every action that doesn't directly lead to results. The business world recognises results, not effort.

### Fact Check

| Claim | Verdict |
|:---|:---|
| Tactical diligence masks strategic laziness | ✅ **Verified** — Lei Jun's famous quote |
| Begin with the end in mind | ✅ **Verified** — Stephen Covey, *The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People*, Habit 2 |
| Business world only recognises results | ✅ **Verified** — but ignores the learning value of process |

### Commentary
✅ **Extremely practical** — Reverse engineering is the core methodology of project management.
✅ **Breaks the "fake effort" trap** — Many people are indeed busy but produce nothing.
⚠️ **Oversimplified** — Some goals (like learning new skills) require process orientation, not simple reverse engineering.

### 🎯 Practical Application at Work

| Work Type | Wrong Approach | Result-Oriented Approach |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Supplier Quality Reports** | "Send a report every day" | "Help the boss understand the Top 3 Supplier issues and solutions this week" |
| **Blog Publishing** | "Publish an article every week" | "What impact should this article have?" → Reverse-engineer: Who is the reader? What do they need? |
| **Cron Management** | "Run crons" | "What automation should this cron achieve?" → Reverse-engineer: Which manual tasks can be automated? |

**Concrete Actions:**
1. **Set 1-3 core results per week** (not task lists)
2. **Reverse-engineer daily actions** — To achieve this result, what must I do today?
3. **Cut work that doesn't lead to results** — Ask: Who will use the output of this cron? If nobody, cut it.

## Habit 3: Energy Management Over Time Management
## 習慣 3：拋棄時間管理，擁抱極致精力管理

**The Claim:** 時間對每個人都是公平的 24 小時，但同樣時間裡的能量輸出天壤之別。高手不追求把每分鐘都填滿，而是找出自己精力最巔峰的 2 小時，用來做深度思考、重要決策、核心技能提升。

**In English:** Time is fair — 24 hours for everyone. But the energy output in the same time varies enormously. Top performers don't try to fill every minute; instead, they find their peak 2 hours of energy and use them for deep thinking, important decisions, and core skill improvement.

### Fact Check

| Claim | Verdict |
|:---|:---|
| Energy management > time management | ✅ **Verified** |
| 2-hour peak window | ✅ **Partially verified** |

**Research:** Jim Loehr & Tony Schwartz, *The Power of Full Engagement* (2003) — Energy has four dimensions: physical, emotional, mental, spiritual. Circadian rhythm studies show most people have cognitive peaks at 9-11 AM and 2-4 PM.

### Commentary
✅ **More fundamental than time management** — The same hour at peak energy can produce 3-5x the output of a tired hour.
✅ **Actionable** — Finding your own energy rhythm is very practical.

### 🎯 Practical Application at Work

| Time Period | Energy Level | Best For |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| **08:00-10:00** | 🟢 Peak | Writing reports, deep analysis, Blog articles |
| **10:00-12:00** | 🟡 High | Replying to boss, handling supplier issues |

| **16:00-18:00** | 🟡 High | Cron debugging, Skill development |

## Habit 4: Extreme Delayed Gratification & Deep Thinking
## 習慣 4：極度反人性的延遲滿足與深度思考

**The Claim:** 在秒懂秒回的快餐時代，所有高回報事物都建立在延遲滿足上。忍受無聊枯燥痛苦去讀透一本晦澀專業書、花半年打磨一個產品，就已經淘汰了 90% 的對手。深度思考是極其痛苦的腦力勞動，大多數人寧死也不願意思考。

**In English:** In the era of instant gratification, all high-ROI pursuits are built on delayed gratification. Enduring boredom, dryness, and pain to thoroughly read an obscure professional book, or spending half a year refining a product — this alone eliminates 90% of competitors. Deep thinking is extremely painful mental labour, and most people would rather die than think.

### Fact Check

| Claim | Verdict |
|:---|:---|
| Delayed gratification is key to success | ✅ **Verified** |
| Deep thinking eliminates 90% of competitors | ✅ **Largely true** |
| Most people refuse to think | ✅ **Verified** |

**Research:** Walter Mischel's Marshmallow Experiment (1972) and 40-year follow-up: children who could delay gratification scored higher on SATs, had lower BMI, and better stress coping. Daniel Kahneman, *Thinking, Fast and Slow*: System 2 (slow thinking) requires significant cognitive resources; the brain naturally prefers System 1 (fast thinking).

### 🎯 Practical Application at Work

| Scenario | Surface Approach | Deep Thinking Approach |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Supplier Audit** | Run through the checklist | Analyse the root problem: capability issue or willingness? Systemic or isolated? |
| **Blog Topic Selection** | Write about trending topics | Research data: Which articles get the most reads? What is the reader profile? |
| **Cron Errors** | Fix the error | Analyse error patterns: sporadic or systemic? Can it be prevented architecturally? |

**Concrete Action:** 30 minutes of "undisturbed thinking" daily — no phone, no research, just sit and think about one problem.

## Habit 5: High-Frequency, Low-Cost Trial and Error
## 習慣 5：高頻次的低成本試錯

**The Claim:** 完美主義是致命弱點。想做項目非要萬事俱備，想拍視頻非要買最貴設備。頂級玩家信奉「先開槍後瞄準」——以最快最低成本把產品扔進市場測試，錯了立刻調整，對了瘋狂放大。

**In English:** Perfectionism is a fatal weakness. Waiting for everything to be perfect before starting a project, buying the most expensive equipment before shooting a video. Top players believe in "shoot first, aim later" — get a product into the market at the fastest, lowest cost, adjust immediately if wrong, and amplify aggressively if right.

### Fact Check

| Claim | Verdict |
|:---|:---|
| Shoot first, aim later | ✅ **Verified** — Lean Startup methodology |
| Failure is the only path to real feedback | ⚠️ **Overly absolute** — Observing others' failures is also a learning path |
| Perfectionism is a weakness | ✅ **Verified** — Psychology research supports this |

**Research:** Eric Ries, *The Lean Startup* — MVP (Minimum Viable Product): validate hypotheses at minimum cost. IDEO Design Thinking: prototype iteration > perfect planning.

### 🎯 Practical Application at Work

| Idea | MVP Approach | Perfectionist Approach (Avoid) |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **New Report Format** | Send a simple table first, ask boss for feedback | Design a perfect template, take 2 weeks before sending |
| **Cron Automation** | Run manually once to verify logic, then automate | Write full automation directly, rewrite when it fails |
| **Blog Article** | Write draft, show boss, then polish and publish | Revise for a month before publishing |

**Concrete Action:** "24-Hour Rule" — Any new idea, produce a testable version within 24 hours.
