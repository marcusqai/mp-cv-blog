## Satya Nadella and the Empathy-Led Culture Change at Microsoft

In 2014, when Satya Nadella became chief executive of Microsoft, the company was facing slowing growth and an internal culture built on competition. Nadella did not treat the turnaround as a product problem or an exercise in redrawing the organization chart. He started with culture: he asked employees to move from being "know-it-alls" to "learn-it-alls", placing curiosity, listening, and empathy at the centre of leadership work.

This was not a slogan that could be delivered in a single speech. He pushed for cross-functional collaboration, made the company more willing to embrace cloud services and different platforms, and re-examined how teams measured success. Once employees were no longer occupied with proving that they were always right, it became easier to admit what they did not know, to ask for help, and to correct course based on feedback from customers and colleagues.

Empathy in this context is not a way of lowering standards. It means understanding the problem the customer actually has, and then responding with better products and better ways of working together. Nadella has also spoken in public interviews about how his own family experience deepened his understanding of listening and care. But cultural change in an organization still depends on daily management systems and everyday behaviour, not on the personal story of one leader.

The transformation carries a reminder for managers: culture is not a slogan on the wall. It is how a team handles mistakes, shares information, listens to dissent, and gives credit for shared results. If you want to build a learning organization, leaders can begin by demonstrating that they do not know everything, and by focusing reviews on the facts, the causes, and the next step rather than rushing to find someone to blame.

## Today's Practice

1. In your next team discussion, first ask: what do we still not know?
2. Then ask: who sees this differently?
3. Finally, turn what you learned into one action that can be verified.

## Key Takeaway

Empathy is a leadership capability, not a soft substitute for accountability. Build a learning culture by listening deeply, admitting what you do not know, and turning feedback into measurable action.

## 薩提亞・納德拉如何以同理心改變微軟

2014 年，薩提亞・納德拉（Satya Nadella）接任微軟執行長時，公司正面對成長放緩與內部競爭文化的挑戰。納德拉沒有只把轉型視為產品或組織圖的調整，而是從文化著手：他鼓勵員工由「無所不知」轉向「不斷學習」，把好奇心、傾聽與同理心放進領導工作的核心。

這並非單靠一句口號就能完成。他推動跨部門合作，讓微軟更願意擁抱雲端服務與不同平台，也重新檢視團隊如何衡量成功。當員工不必忙於證明自己永遠正確，便更容易承認未知、尋求協助，並從客戶和同事的回饋中修正方向。

同理心在這裡不是放低標準，而是理解客戶真正遇到的問題，再以更好的產品與合作方式回應。納德拉也在公開訪談中談到，個人家庭經歷使他更深刻體會傾聽與理解的重要；但企業文化的改變，仍須靠日常管理制度與行為持續支持，而不是只依賴領導者的個人故事。

這段轉型提醒管理者：文化不是牆上的標語，而是團隊如何處理錯誤、分享資訊、聽取異議，以及把功勞歸給共同成果。若要建立學習型組織，領導者可以先示範承認不知道，並在檢討時聚焦於事實、原因和下一步，而非急著找人背責。

## 今日實踐

1. 在下一次團隊討論中，先問：「我們還不知道什麼？」
2. 再問：「誰有不同觀點？」
3. 最後，把學到的事轉成一項可驗證的行動。

## 核心啟示

同理心是一種領導能力，而不是問責的替代品。要建立學習文化，領導者需要深入傾聽、承認自己不知道的事，並把回饋轉化為可衡量的行動。
