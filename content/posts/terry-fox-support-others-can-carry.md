## Terry Fox: The Man Who Stopped, and the People Who Carried On

On 12 April 1980, at St John's, Newfoundland, a young Canadian named Terry Fox dipped the foot of his artificial leg into the Atlantic Ocean and began to run. He had lost his right leg to cancer. The run was not a race with a finish medal. It was a campaign to raise money for cancer research.

For 143 days he covered 5,373 kilometres across Canada. Support along the way grew steadily, and so did the donations. His goal was easy for anyone to understand: one dollar for every Canadian.

On 1 September the run stopped near Thunder Bay, Ontario. The cancer had spread to his lungs and he returned for treatment. He did not complete the planned route across the country.

That fact should not be quietly removed from the story, and the story should not be turned into a claim that sufficient willpower makes illness step aside. What happened next was not that he started running again. It was that other people took up the work. On 7 September, five hours of national television raised ten million dollars. By February of the following year the fund had reached 24.17 million dollars, meeting the original one-dollar-per-Canadian target.

Terry Fox died in June 1981. He was twenty-two. On 13 September that same year, the first run in his name took place, with roughly three hundred thousand participants. An effort that began with one person became something many people could join.

Management and life lessons, this is our analysis.

The most useful thing in this account is not a demand that everyone push on while ill. It is the design of the support itself. A campaign one person started could be continued by others, because the purpose was stated plainly, the progress was visible, and the way to join was obvious. Anyone could run a segment. Anyone could donate. Nobody needed a personal relationship with Fox to understand what to do.

That is a management problem as much as a moral one. When a project depends entirely on one person, stopping is a disappearance. When the purpose is written down, the current state is visible, and the contribution required is clear, stopping becomes a handover. A person can rest without the work collapsing.

In practice this means three things. Say precisely what the work is for, in one sentence a stranger could repeat. Publish the current position honestly, including what has not been achieved. And make the next unit of contribution small enough that someone else can pick it up without a long briefing.

This is a limited analogy and should be read as one. It does not reduce illness to a project risk, and it does not ask anyone to carry a burden that has become physically unbearable. The transferable idea is narrower: the value of what we build is partly measured by whether it survives our absence.

## Today's Practice

1. Write the purpose of one ongoing commitment in a single sentence someone else could repeat.
2. State honestly where it stands today — including the part that is not going well.
3. Define one small piece another person could take over this week, and tell them so.

## Key Takeaway

Support is not always an instruction to keep going. Sometimes it is taking over part of the work so that the other person can stop safely. An effort that others can continue is worth more than one that only works while you are present.

## 福克斯：停下來的人，與接下去的人

一九八零年四月十二日，加拿大青年泰瑞・福克斯在紐芬蘭聖約翰斯，把義肢的腳浸入大西洋，然後開始長跑。他曾因癌症失去右腿。這不是一場有終點獎牌的競賽，而是為癌症研究籌款的行動。

接下來一百四十三天，他跑過五千三百七十三公里，橫越加拿大。沿途支持的人越來越多，捐款也逐步增加。他的目標很容易理解：為每一位加拿大人籌得一加元。

九月一日，長跑在安大略省雷灣附近停止。癌症已擴散至肺部，他返回接受治療，沒有完成原定的橫越全國路線。

這個事實不應在故事中被悄悄刪去，也不應把故事說成只要意志足夠堅定，疾病就會退讓。接下來的轉折不是他再次起跑，而是其他人接下了這件事。九月七日，五小時的全國電視籌款活動籌得一千萬加元。到了次年二月，基金款項達到二千四百一十七萬加元，實現了原本「每位加拿大人一加元」的目標。

福克斯在一九八一年六月去世，年僅二十二歲。同年九月十三日，首屆以他命名的長跑舉行，約三十萬人參加。一件由一個人開始的事，變成很多人可以參與的事。

管理與生活啟示，以下是本報分析。

這段經歷中最有用的地方，不是要求每個人在患病時仍要硬撐，而是支援本身的設計。一個人發起的行動之所以能由他人延續，是因為目的說得清楚、進度看得見、參與方式明顯。任何人都可以跑一段路，任何人都可以捐款。不需要與福克斯有私人關係，也能明白自己可以做甚麼。

這既是管理問題，也是道德問題。當一個項目完全依賴某一個人，停下來就等於消失。當目的寫了下來、當前狀況公開、需要付出的貢獻具體，停下來就變成交接。人可以休息，事情不會隨之崩塌。

落實在日常，有三件事：用一句陌生人也能複述的話，說清楚這件事為了甚麼；誠實公布目前的位置，包括尚未達成的部分；把下一份貢獻切得足夠小，讓別人不必經過冗長交代就能接手。

這只是有限的類比。它不把疾病簡化為項目風險，也不要求任何人承擔已到身體極限的重擔。可以帶走的想法較窄：我們所建立的事，其價值有一部分取決於它能否在我們不在場時繼續存在。

## 今日實踐

1. 為一項仍在進行的承諾，寫下一句別人可以複述的目的。
2. 誠實寫出它今天的位置，包括進行得不順的部分。
3. 界定一件別人本週就可以接手的小事，並告訴對方。

## 核心啟示

支持不一定等於叫對方繼續堅持。有時是接過其中一部分，讓對方可以安全地停下來。一件別人能夠接續的事，比一件只在你親自在場時才成立的事，更有價值。
